\section{Methods}

Our corpus contains \dsize{} passages, each truncated to a context budget of
\unit{80}{token} per passage and a wall-clock budget of \unit{4}{min} per
query during evaluation. \todo{Add the $m = 20$ run} once the larger sweep
finishes.

We tune the BM25 scoring function with $k_1 \in \{0.9, 1.2, 1.5\}$ and
$b = 0.75$, matching the retrieval-matched compute protocol of
\citep[cf.][]{lindqvist2021}. The scoring function itself is given in
\eqref{eq:bm25} and is not re-derived here.

\begin{itemize}
\item Passages are chunked with a 10\% overlap between adjacent windows.
\item Embeddings are computed once per collection and cached on disk.
\item Retrieval quality is reported at matched compute, not matched wall
      time, per \citet{lindqvist2021}.
\end{itemize}
